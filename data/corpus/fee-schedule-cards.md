# Fee Schedule — Cards
doc_id: FEE-03
doc_type: fee_schedule

## [FEE-03-1] Card Replacement Fee
A fee of $10 applies for expedited replacement of a lost or stolen debit or credit card; standard replacement is free.

## [FEE-03-2] Cash Advance Fee
A cash advance fee of 5% of the amount advanced (minimum $10) applies to credit card cash advances.
