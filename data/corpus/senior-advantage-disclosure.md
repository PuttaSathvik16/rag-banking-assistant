# Senior Advantage Savings — Product Disclosure
doc_id: ACCT-04
doc_type: account_product

## [ACCT-04-1] Product Overview
The Senior Advantage Savings account is a retail deposit product offered for personal banking customers.

## [ACCT-04-2] Minimum Balance Requirement
A minimum daily balance of $1000 must be maintained to avoid a maintenance fee.

## [ACCT-04-3] Monthly Maintenance Fee
A monthly maintenance fee of $0.00 applies if the minimum balance requirement is not met. The fee is waived for customers enrolled in direct deposit of at least $500/month.

## [ACCT-04-4] Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years of age (or a co-signer for Senior Advantage Savings if under 18), a U.S. resident, and provide valid government-issued identification.

## [ACCT-04-5] Interest / APY
Where applicable, interest accrues daily and is credited monthly. Rates are variable and disclosed at account opening.
