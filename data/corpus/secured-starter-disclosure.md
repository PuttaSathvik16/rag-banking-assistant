# Secured Starter Card — Product Disclosure
doc_id: CARD-02
doc_type: card_product

## [CARD-02-1] Card Overview
The Secured Starter Card is a consumer credit card product.

## [CARD-02-2] Annual Fee
An annual fee of $0 applies, billed on the account anniversary.

## [CARD-02-3] Standard APR
The standard purchase APR is 24.99%, variable, based on the Prime Rate plus a margin disclosed at account opening.

## [CARD-02-4] Late Payment Fee
A late payment fee of up to $25 applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

## [CARD-02-5] Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years of age, have a valid Social Security Number or ITIN, and meet minimum credit criteria at underwriting.

## [CARD-02-6] Foreign Transaction Fee
A foreign transaction fee of 3% of the transaction amount applies to purchases made outside the United States, except where the card explicitly waives this fee.
