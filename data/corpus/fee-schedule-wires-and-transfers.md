# Fee Schedule — Wires & Transfers
doc_id: FEE-02
doc_type: fee_schedule

## [FEE-02-1] Wire Transfer — Domestic Outgoing
A fee of $25 applies to domestic outgoing wire transfers.

## [FEE-02-2] Wire Transfer — International Outgoing
A fee of $45 applies to international outgoing wire transfers.

## [FEE-02-3] ATM Fee — Out of Network
A fee of $3.00 applies per withdrawal at an out-of-network ATM, in addition to any fee charged by the ATM owner.
