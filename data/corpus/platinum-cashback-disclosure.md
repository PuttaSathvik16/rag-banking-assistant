# Platinum Cashback Mastercard — Product Disclosure
doc_id: CARD-03
doc_type: card_product

## [CARD-03-1] Card Overview
The Platinum Cashback Mastercard is a consumer credit card product.

## [CARD-03-2] Annual Fee
An annual fee of $95 applies, billed on the account anniversary.

## [CARD-03-3] Standard APR
The standard purchase APR is 19.99%, variable, based on the Prime Rate plus a margin disclosed at account opening.

## [CARD-03-4] Late Payment Fee
A late payment fee of up to $40 applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

## [CARD-03-5] Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years of age, have a valid Social Security Number or ITIN, and meet minimum credit criteria at underwriting.

## [CARD-03-6] Foreign Transaction Fee
A foreign transaction fee of 3% of the transaction amount applies to purchases made outside the United States, except where the card explicitly waives this fee.
