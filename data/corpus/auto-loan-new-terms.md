# Auto Loan — New Vehicle — Product Terms
doc_id: LOAN-02
doc_type: loan_product

## [LOAN-02-1] Product Overview
The Auto Loan — New Vehicle is a secured consumer lending product for personal use.

## [LOAN-02-2] Interest Rate Range
APR ranges from 5.49% to 14.99%, based on creditworthiness, loan term, and (for auto loans) vehicle age.

## [LOAN-02-3] Origination Fee
No origination fee applies to this loan product.

## [LOAN-02-4] Foreclosure / Early Payoff Charge
Loans may be paid off early at any time with no prepayment penalty. A foreclosure processing charge of $75 applies only if the loan is closed via foreclosure proceedings following default.

## [LOAN-02-5] Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years of age, a U.S. resident, and meet the bank's minimum credit score and debt-to-income thresholds published in the Eligibility Policy Manual.

## [LOAN-02-6] Term Length
Terms are available from 24 to 72 months, subject to loan type and amount.
