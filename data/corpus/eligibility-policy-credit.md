# Eligibility Policy Manual — Credit Underwriting
doc_id: ELIG-02
doc_type: policy_manual

## [ELIG-02-1] Credit Score Thresholds
Unsecured credit products (credit cards, personal loans) generally require a minimum credit score of 640. Secured products (Secured Starter Card, auto loans) have no minimum score but require income verification.

## [ELIG-02-2] Debt-to-Income Threshold
Loan applications are generally declined if the applicant's debt-to-income ratio exceeds 45%, subject to underwriter discretion.

## [ELIG-02-3] Adverse Action Notice
Applicants who are declined will receive a written adverse action notice within 30 days, per Regulation B, stating the principal reasons for denial.
