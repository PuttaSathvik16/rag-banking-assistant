# Fee Schedule — Deposit Accounts
doc_id: FEE-01
doc_type: fee_schedule

## [FEE-01-1] Overdraft Fee
An overdraft fee of $34 applies per item that overdraws the account, up to 3 items per day.

## [FEE-01-2] Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) Fee
An NSF fee of $34 applies when a transaction is declined due to insufficient funds.

## [FEE-01-3] Paper Statement Fee
A fee of $3 per month applies for paper statements unless the customer is enrolled in e-statements.

## [FEE-01-4] Stop Payment Fee
A fee of $30 applies per stop-payment request on a check.
